What Documents Do You Need?

We guide you based on your tax category, income type, and filing status.

Personal & Identity
  • Passport and visa copy

Income Documents
  • Form W-2
  • Form 1099
  • Form 1042-S
  • Business or freelance records
Financial & Deductions
  • 1099-INT, 1099-DIV, 1099-B
  • Mortgage and education forms
  • Donation and childcare records
